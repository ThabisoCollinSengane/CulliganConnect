## How to find the right *person* (without scraping)
LinkedIn search (people), filtered by company, using these titles:
- `"Learning and Development" OR "L&D" OR "Training Manager" OR "HR Manager"` + company name
- For providers: `"Academic Director" OR "Director of Studies" OR "Operations Manager" OR "Founder"`
- For BPOs: `"Training Manager" OR "Quality Manager" OR "Operations Director"`
Send a **personalised connection note** (≤300 characters), never a mass message. Use the company's
website contact form when you can't find the role. Log every touch in `tracker.csv`
(`status`: CONTACTED → REPLIED → CALL BOOKED → PILOT → WON / LOST).
