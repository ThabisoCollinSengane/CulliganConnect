## The one-paragraph answer
Start as a **one-person business selling small-group corporate English programmes, not hours.**
Price **per group-hour (US$32 for 4–8 adults)**, so each learner pays only about US$4–8, which is
cheaper than 1:1 marketplace lessons, while you earn about 3.7× your eventual cost per hour. Sell
**prepaid monthly packages** with minimum hours, so utilisation is the client's problem, not yours.
Lead in **Vietnam**, add Thailand, and treat China adults as a third wave via a partner. Hire your
first tutor **part-time and hourly** once hours are signed, and go full-time on R16,000 only when a
tutor's signed hours fill a salary. On these prices, a 5-tutor remote team breaks even at about **40%
utilisation**. At the plan's US$12 1:1 price, the same team **loses money even at 70%**.

**Why group pricing fixes most of the problems:**
- It passes your 2.0× rule with room to spare.
- The per-learner price is lower than Filipino 1:1, so you're not in a race to the bottom.
- One booked hour earns 2–3× more, so lower utilisation hurts far less.

## 3. Pricing rules (put these in every proposal and contract)
1. **Floor:** never sell a core hour below 2.0× cost per billable hour. At current inputs that's
   **US$16.87** (calculator `Inputs!B18`). Only the language-centre filler may go lower, and never below 1.5×.
2. **Quote in US$, invoice monthly in advance** (packages prepaid). No work starts before payment.
3. **Minimum monthly hours** per contract (take-or-pay). Unused hours expire monthly (allow max 10% rollover).
4. **24-hour cancellation:** a late cancel or learner no-show is billed.
5. **Price lock for 12 months, then the greater of CPI + 3% or 5% per year.** This replaces the
   US$12 → US$18 schedule, which buyers are likely to reject (CHALLENGE §6). New clients always pay
   the current list price.
6. **Discounts:** max 10%, only for 6-month prepayment. Never discount the pilot.
7. **Foreign taxes are the client's:** "prices exclude any taxes withheld at source; client grosses up".
8. **Network clause** for cross-border connectivity outages outside your control.
