# R1 — Platform terms: can an agency run tutors through Cambly / Preply / italki / Native Camp?
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08 · **Author:** market-researcher (first pass) · **Status:** DRAFT – awaiting fact-check

## Summary
On the evidence found, **no**. italki states plainly that a teacher can have only one account and
can't share it. Cambly pays only into an account the tutor personally holds rights to. No
agency/partner programme was found for any platform. Preply, Native Camp, AmazingTalker and Engoo
terms weren't confirmed this pass. This confirms HANDOVER v2 §1: marketplaces are for learning the
craft only.
