## What the name needs to do
1. Read as a **professional B2B training supplier** to an HR/L&D manager in Hanoi, Shanghai or Bangkok.
2. Be easy to say and spell for non-native speakers (avoid clicks, "Kh", "Xh").
3. Not imply credentials you don't have (no "Academy accredited", "Institute", "Cambridge").
4. Leave room for a Chinese and a Vietnamese trading name later.
5. Tell the Durban/Ubuntu story on the About page, not necessarily in the name.
