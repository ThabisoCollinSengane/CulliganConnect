# R6 — South African legal & compliance briefing (for accountant / attorney)
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08 · **Author:** market-researcher + ops-planner (first pass) · **Status:** DRAFT
> **Not legal or tax advice.** This is a briefing to take to an accountant and an attorney.

## Summary
Registering a Pty Ltd costs **R175** at CIPC (R125 registration + R50 name reservation). Employed tutors
on R16,000/month are **below the BCEA earnings threshold (R269,600.90/yr from 1 May 2026)**, so full BCEA
protections apply, including overtime. The LRA s200A presumption of employment also applies, which
supports the decision to employ rather than contract. Services exported to non-resident clients may be
**zero-rated for VAT under s11(2)(l)**, but only with the right documents. UIF and SDL apply. Child
safeguarding largely drops away under the adults-only scope, but POPIA still applies to learner and
tutor data.

## Checklist (when the Phase 3 gate is reached, not before)
- [ ] Name decided → CIPC name reservation (R50) → registration (R125) — **owner approval**
- [ ] Business bank account with forex receiving capability (R7)
- [ ] SARS: income tax (auto on registration), PAYE/UIF/SDL employer registration (EMP101e)
- [ ] VAT: voluntary vs compulsory; zero-rating documentation pack for foreign clients
- [ ] COIDA registration
- [ ] Employment contract template (40 hrs, overtime 1.5×/2×, bonus terms, fixed-term option)
- [ ] Client contract template (minimum hours, cancellation, prepayment, tax gross-up, network clause)
- [ ] POPIA: Information Officer, privacy notice, data-processing terms with platforms

## Questions for the attorney
1. Can pilot tutors be on **fixed-term contracts tied to a specific client contract** (LRA s198B)?
2. Are retention/attendance bonuses discretionary or contractual, and how should they be worded?
3. Night/early shifts for Asian daytime training: what BCEA obligations apply?
4. Does delivering online English to **adults in China** from SA create any legal exposure for the SA
   company? What should the client contract say about compliance with Chinese law? (China K-12 stays a BLOCKER regardless.)
5. Governing law and dispute forum for contracts with Vietnamese and Chinese companies.
