# R4 — Pricing benchmarks (adult / corporate, China + Vietnam + Thailand)
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08 · **Author:** market-researcher (first pass) · **Status:** DRAFT – client-side rates still UNVERIFIED

## Summary (≤5 lines)
No public **client-side** rate card was found for B2B online English in China, Vietnam or Thailand.
What exists is **teacher-pay** data, which sets a floor under what clients pay. Vietnam in-person
corporate teacher pay (~US$21–38/hr) is well above the plan's US$12–14 client rate. Online 1:1 teacher
pay is lower (~US$12–15/hr). China figures are anecdotal and old. Real client prices need direct quotes
(see "How to get real numbers").

## Reading the numbers
- **Teacher pay floor ≠ client price.** A provider paying a Hanoi trainer US$25/hr in person charges
  the client more. So a *managed online* programme at US$16–20/hr may be competitive in Vietnam
  corporate. That's a hypothesis to test, not a fact.
- **Online 1:1 pay (~US$12–15)** is the price band where Filipino supply competes. Selling at US$12
  puts the agency head-to-head on price with no margin buffer (see CHALLENGE §1).

## How to get real client-side numbers (ethical, no fake enquiries)
1. Ask in genuine discovery calls with prospects: "What are you paying today, and per what unit?"
2. Public tender documents (Vietnam VNEPS) often show awarded contract values. Search Vietnamese terms
   (đào tạo tiếng Anh doanh nghiệp).
3. Published course prices of corporate-English providers' public packages (per-course, divide by hours).
Do **not** pose as a buyer to get quotes from competitors.

## Recommendation
TEST three price points in the first 10 conversations: **US$16 managed corporate**, **US$13
language-centre subcontract with minimum hours**, **US$10 paid pilot (90 min, step-up written in)**.
